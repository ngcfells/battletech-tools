## Weapon: Armor-Piercing
### Data Extract Profile
==Description==
'''Armor-Piercing''' ammunition is used solely in [[Autocannon|standard]] and [[Light Autocannon|light autocannons]]. It was first prototyped by the [[Federated Suns]] in [[3053]], with full production beginning in [[3059]].  AP ammo uses advanced ballistics materials and improved anti-armor warheads, allowing them to punch through otherwise fresh [[Armor (BattleMechs & Combat Vehicles)|armor]] and damage internal systems. However, their increased weight negatively affects the weapon's accuracy and allows for less ammunition to be stored.<ref name="TMp208">''TechManual'', p. 208, "Autocannon Munitions - Armor-Piercing Ammunition"</ref>

## Weapon: Caseless
### Data Extract Profile
==Description==
'''Caseless''' autocannon ammunition was developed by the [[Federated Commonwealth]] in [[3055]]<ref name="Top352">''Tactical Operations'', p. 352, "Caseless Autocannon Ammo"</ref> to provide more ammunition. Instead of a standard metal or plastic shell casing filled with propellant, the [[:Category:Caseless Autocannon|caseless autocannon]] round had the propellant "packed" around the projectile. This resulted in lighter autocannon rounds, allowing a 'Mech's autocannon magazine to carry twice as many rounds. This feature was particularly useful for the larger bore autocannon, like the [[Autocannon 20]] mounted on the ''[[Hunchback]]''.

Though the caseless rounds were effective most of the time in some notable cases they would jam the 'Mech's ammunition feed system, leaving the MechWarrior in a fight without their biggest gun. The other problem is that the [[NAIS]] wasn't able to create a feed system that could handle caseless and standard ammunition. This meant that an autocannon capable of firing caseless rounds could only fire caseless rounds. Given the relative scarcity of such ammunition, and that the cannon couldn't use ammunition captured from other armies, the caseless capable autocannon remains very rare on the field.<ref name="Top352"/>

## Weapon: Flak
### Data Extract Profile
==Description==
Developed by the [[Terran Hegemony]] around [[2310]]<ref name="TOp352">''Tactical Operations'', p. 352, "Flak Autocannon Ammo"</ref>, '''Flak''' autocannon rounds are designed to be used in anti-aircraft units like the ''[[Rifleman]]'' or ''[[Partisan]]'' tank. This ammunition uses proximity charges to detonate in midair. While potent against fast-moving targets susceptible to foreign object damage (FOD), ﬂak ammo is less effective against slower-moving targets on the ground because the charges scatter their shrapnel too far and too quickly to beneﬁt from the target’s mobility.

However, the fragments generated by flak ammunition are too small to inflict significant damage on other combat units; it is partly the motion of the VTOL or fighter through the flak cloud that helps inflict the damage. The ineffectiveness against ground armored units is the reason why this type of ammunition is quite unpopular on today’s battlefield.<ref name="TOp352"/>

The only exception is against conventional infantry where the fast moving shrapnel turns flak rounds into a sort of "poor man's" Flechette round but it's considered a desperate at best to wasteful at worse, to use it in such a manner.

## Weapon: Flechette Ammo
### Data Extract Profile
{{Orphan}}
==Description==
'''Flechette''' ammunition is a type of special round used for slug-throwing weapons.  Rather than a bullet it releases a spray of tiny darts which are very effective against unarmored or lightly-armored personnel and virtually useless against armored targets. In [[CBT]] game terms, Flechette ammunition does double damage against conventional infantry and woods but half damage to all other targets. They may only be loaded in standard and light autocannons.

## Weapon: Incendiary
### Data Extract Profile
==Description==
'''Incendiary''' ammunition can be used in standard [[autocannon]]s and their [[Light Autocannon|lighter cousins]].  Developed by [[NAIS]] scientists from the [[Federated Suns]], these autocannon rounds contained an inferno warhead and were covered in a thin coat of magnesium.  Upon leaving the barrel the magnesium would ignite and continue to burn until the material was completely consumed.  Besides the additional benefit of setting targets aflame, this also aided the firer during low-visibility combat, although the volatile ammunition could be just as dangerous to them as the enemy.<ref name="FM:FSp159">''Field Manual: Federated Suns'', p. 159, "Incendiary Ammunition"</ref>

## Weapon: Precision ammunition
### Data Extract Profile
==Description==
'''Precision''' ammunition can be used in standard [[autocannon]]s and their [[Light Autocannon|lighter cousins]]. Scientists from the [[Federated Suns]] managed to modify autocannon ammunition to accommodate a gyrojet shell that can correct its course in mid-flight, but the course correction system increased the mass of each shell. Debuting in [[3062]],<ref name="TMp207+">''TechManual'', pp. 207-208, "Autocannon"</ref> precision ammo soon found its way into the armies of other nations.

== Rules ==
<div class="gamerules">
=== Game Rules ===
In [[CBT]] play, precision ammunition grants the firer a 2 point reduction to the Target Movement Modifier. This comes at a cost, however, of only being able to carry half as many rounds per ton (round down).<ref name="TWp142">''Total Warfare'', p. 142, "Precision Ammunition"</ref><ref name="FM:FSp159">''Field Manual: Federated Suns'', p. 159, "Precision Ammunition"</ref>

## Weapon: Tracer
### Data Extract Profile
{{Merge|Tracer Ammo}}

==Description==
'''Tracer''' ammunition was first developed by the [[Terran Hegemony]] in [[2300]]. For use in [[autocannon]]s, it was used to coordinate fire during nighttime operations, with self-illuminating "tracer" rounds interspersed among the normal ammunition.  However, the popularity of cheap, mass-produced firepower led to a decline in tracer ammunition.  Modern autocannons, if they bother to arm themselves with tracer rounds, tend to load one tracer round between fifty normal rounds.<ref name="TOp353">''Tactical Operations'' p.v353, "Tracer Autocannon Ammo"</ref>

== Rules ==
<div class="gamerules">
=== Game Rules ===
In game terms, Tracer ammo is equivalent to standard ammo in terms of weight and range.  It inflicts -1 point of damage per shot (down to a minimum of 1) while at the same time reducing to-hit modifiers for night combat to +1 and eliminating dusk/dawn modifiers.<ref name="TOp353"/>
</div>
