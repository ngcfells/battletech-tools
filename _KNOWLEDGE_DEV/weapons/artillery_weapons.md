==Description==
The '''Arrow IV''' is a stand-alone, missile-based artillery system, designed to augment conventional systems such as the [[Long Tom Artillery Piece|Long Tom]] and the [[Sniper Artillery Piece|Sniper]]. It was first introduced by the [[Terran Hegemony]] in [[2593]] and became a staple of the [[Star League Defense Force]] by [[2600]].<ref name="TOp284">''Tactical Operations: Advanced Units & Equipment'', p. 165: "Artillery - Arrow IV Artillery Missile"</ref>  <ref name="IOp70">''Interstellar Operations'', p. 70: "Arrow-P"</ref> Although the technology to create Arrow IVs was lost during the Star League's collapse, it was recovered by [[Capellan Confederation]] engineers in [[3044]] working off of surviving examples and the [[Helm Memory Core]].  

Arrow IV launchers can be equipped with standard unguided area saturation missiles, causing massive damage to any unit within its 45-meter blast radius, or [[Arrow IV Homing Missile|homing missiles]] which work in concert with forward units carrying [[Target Acquisition Gear]] to lessen collateral damage. The Prototype (known as the Arrow-P) version is bulkier and heavier than a standard Arrow IV launcher by a ton, and can only carry 4 shots per ton of conventional homing ammo.<ref name="IOp70">''Interstellar Operations'', p. 70: "Arrow-P"</ref> The Clan version is lighter than both, and can also deliver a '''FASCAM''' (Field Artillery Scatterable Mines) round.<ref name="BT:MRp123+">''BattleTech Master Rules'', pp. 123–124: "Artillery Weapons - Arrow IV Artillery Missile System"</ref>

==Description==
The '''Battle Armor Tube Artillery''' (often simple '''BA Tube Artillery''') is an [[infantry]] scale [[artillery]] piece designed for use with [[battle armor]]. The weapon was introduced during the [[Jihad]], but it's unclear by whom.<ref name="TOpp284+" />

== Rules ==
<div class="gamerules">
=== Game Rules ===
==== Gameplay ====
BA Artillery uses following standard rules that normal conventional [[artillery]] weapons use; Indirect, Direct Artillery, and Counter-Battery Fire. They can use alternate ammunition that the standard conventional type may use.  It should be noted that the BA Tube Artillery is fired by a [[Squad]] and damage is multiplied based on the remaining active battle armor trooper.<ref name="TOpp284+" />

==== Construction Rules====
The weapon ammunition weighs 15 kg for 2 rounds and the entire weapon takes up 4 spaces in a Battle Armor.  The weapon is an area effect weapon with switchable ammunition. Ammunition for the BA Tube Artillery must be purchased in 1-ton lots. While being constructed using battle armor construction rules, BA Artillery ammunition is treated the same as Missile-based ammunition. Like the Missile based weapons for a Battle Armor, each mag of 2-rounds is treated as a single shot.  Placement for the weapon is always located in the body of the armor. In [[3075]], the weapon system itself uses Experimental rules, while use of Artillery is listed as being [[Rules_Level#Advanced_Rules|Advanced Rules]].<ref name="TOpp284+" />
</div>

==Notes==
The name '''BA Tube Artillery''' is the official name used for this weapon. There is limited information regarding the BA Tube Artillery's background and development aside from brief descriptions found in the ''[[Tactical Operations]]'' Conventional Artillery section/construction rules. The only known image of the weapon is seen on the [[Centaur Battle Armor]] picture shown in the infobox.

## Weapon: Cruise Missile
### Data Extract Profile
==Description==

Until the [[Federated Suns]] started researching them, the '''Cruise Missile''' had been assigned to history. Basically a large version of the unguided [[Arrow IV Artillery|Arrow IV]] missile, the [[Armed Forces of the Federated Suns]] began testing the Cruise Missile in 3065. Unlike the Arrow IV missile system, there is no "guided" or "homing" cruise missile. This relative inaccuracy offsets the tremendous damage they cause.<ref name=to284>''Tactical Operations'', pp. 284-285 - Cruise Missile (Basic info) - Basic information.</ref> What cruise missiles lack in precision however, they make up for in sheer terror. Even at the tactical level the effect of massive warheads impacting without warning on the battlefield can make even the most experienced and capable units break and flee. <ref name="TOp211">''Tactical Operations'', pp. 211-212, Morale Checks - (Cruise Missiles Attack)</ref>

These missiles are truly staggering in size, the lightest launcher weighing in at 55 tons <ref name="TOp405">''Tactical Operations'', p. 405, "Heavy Weapons And Equipment Construction Data"</ref>. So far only the [[Cruise Missile 50]] has received a production run; for all other models no information about their deployment is known. Due to the lower cost and greater maneuverability of [[Capital Missile]] Launchers, the largest variants were unlikely to be used frequently.<ref name=to284>''Tactical Operations'', pp. 284-285 - Cruise Missile (Basic info) - Basic information.</ref>

## Weapon: Field Artillery
### Data Extract Profile
==Description==
'''Field Artillery''', in its present form was introduced around [[2200]]. It consists of [[artillery]] pieces that are towed into battle by mechanized conventional infantry. ## Weapon: Field Gun
### Data Extract Profile
[[File:Field Gun.jpg|190px|right|thumb|Field Gun]]
==Description==
'''Field Guns''' are vehicular-scale ballistic weapons, such as [[autocannon]]s and [[gauss rifle]]s, which are towed into battle by motorized or mechanized conventional infantry. These weapons greatly increase the firepower of conventional infantry, however their exposed nature leaves them more vulnerable to enemy fire.<ref name=TO311>''Tactical Operations'', p. 311</ref> 

==Notes==
* Hover-based mechanized infantry may not be equipped with field guns.<ref name=TO311/>
* Gauss field guns are immune to weapon explosion effects.<ref name=TO311/>
* [[Ultra Autocannon|Ultra]] and [[Rotary Autocannon|Rotary]] field guns are immune to jamming. <ref name=TO311/>
* [[LB-X Autocannon|LB-X]] field guns may only use cluster ammunition, while [[Heavy Gauss Rifle|Heavy]] or [[Hyper-Assault Gauss Rifle|Hyper-Assault]] gauss rifles may not be taken as field guns.<ref name=TO311/> 
* Game rules can be found in [[Tactical Operations: Advanced Units & Equipment]]<ref name=TO311/><ref name=TOAU123>''Tactical Operations: Advanced Units & Equipment'', p. 123</ref>

==Description==
The '''Long Tom Artillery Piece''' is the largest conventional artillery piece designed by [[Armstrong Industries]]. It is the primary weapon of the [[Mobile Long Tom|LT-MOB-25 Mobile Long Tom Artillery]] vehicle, which is better known as the Mobile Long Tom.<ref name=TRO:3025Rp142>''Technical Readout: 3025 Revised'', p. 142: "LB-MOB-25 Moble Long Tom Artillery"</ref>

It was also the primary heavy artillery unit of the first [[Star League Defense Force]].<ref name="TRO:2750p102">''Technical Readout: 2750'', p. 102: "Chaparral"</ref>

A Long Tom III artillery piece is also mounted in the top (forward) section of ''[[Fortress]]'' class [[DropShip]]s, giving them significant firepower on the ground.

== Game Rules ==
The Long Tom is the largest [[Artillery|artillery]] piece that can be mounted on a vehicle, taking up 15 critical slots on a support vehicle. It also has the longest range of any non–[[Cruise Missile]] type weapon. It deals the highest damage of conventional tube artillery, at 25 damage with direct impact using conventional (high-explosive) ammunition.

The Long Tom artillery's ammunition options include [[Smoke Shell|Smoke]], Anti-Personnel, [[Illumination Shell|Illumination]], [[Copperhead Shell|Copperhead]], [[Flechette Shell|Flechette]], [[Thunder Shell|Thunder/FASCAM]], [[Cluster Shell|Cluster]], and High Explosive Rounds (High Explosive rounds considered the "Standard" rounds for tube artillery).

The Long Tom can also fire Davy Crockett-M tactical nuclear devices but these are significantly heavier than normal conventional rounds (represented in game rules by being one shot per ton unlike normal rounds) but other than their nuclear weapon effects behave like normal rounds.

==Description==
The '''Sniper Artillery Piece''' is the smaller cousin to the [[Long Tom Artillery Piece]] designed by [[Armstrong Industries]].  As the second most-powerful tube artillery in the [[Inner Sphere]], the Sniper can cripple or destroy most light and medium [[BattleMechs]] with a single hit.  The weapon has an effective range of 9 kilometers.  While usable as [[Field Artillery|field artillery]], the Sniper is also mounted on several vehicles: the [[Marksman Artillery Vehicle|Marksman]], [[Padilla Tube Artillery Tank]], [[Sniper Artillery (Combat Vehicle)|Sniper Artillery Tank]], and [[Teppō]].  It was also mounted on the [[BattleMech]] [[Helepolis]], though the 'Mech proved ineffective as an artillery mount.

==Description==
The '''Thumper''' Artillery Piece is the smallest conventional artillery piece with an effective range of 10.5 kilometers.<ref name=TO181>''Tactical Operations'', p. 181</ref>  While an effective [[Field Artillery|field artillery]] weapon, the Thumper is perhaps best known for its use as the main gun on the [[SLDF]]'s [[Thor (Combat Vehicle)|Thor Artillery Vehicle]], as well as the Brooks Incorporated knockoff vehicle, the [[Thumper (Combat Vehicle)|Thumper]].  It is later mounted on the [[Danai|Danai Support Vehicle]] and a variant of the [[Padilla Tube Artillery Tank]], as well as the [[Cloud Ten|Cloud Ten Airship Carrier]] and [[Schwerer Gustav]] [[FrankenMech]]. The Jihad-era T-IT-N14R variant of the [[Grand Titan]] Battlemech also mounts a Thumper.
