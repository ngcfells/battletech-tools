Lets explore how penta and hexa would work logically and whether we could legitimally come up with AlphaStrike rules that would be accepted by the community for them...

To make penta-legged (5) and hexa-legged (6) 'Mechs work logically and feel "legitimate" to the BattleTech community, they need to follow the existing engineering philosophy of the universe. In BattleTech, adding more legs is a tradeoff: you gain stability, weight-bearing capacity, and redundant systems, but you lose speed, internal space, and mechanical simplicity.Here is a logical engineering breakdown and a draft for balanced Alpha Strike rules that the community would likely accept.

Part 1: The In-Universe Logic & EngineeringThe Hexapod (6 Legs)The Logic: A hexapod uses a alternating tripod gait (moving three legs at a time), which is incredibly stable and allows it to traverse brutal terrain without losing balance.The Tradeoff: Six legs mean six massive hip actuators and heavy structural reinforcement. This eats up internal space and tonnage. A hexapod wouldn't be fast; it would be a walking fortress, a mobile turret, or a specialized combat engineer vehicle designed for extreme environments (like deep underwater or high-gravity worlds).The Pentapod (5 Legs)The Logic: BattleTech already has Tripods (3 legs). If a Tripod loses a leg, it is crippled. A Pentapod could lose a leg and seamlessly transition to a Quad gait, or lose two legs and still walk as a Tripod.The Tradeoff: Five is an asymmetrical nightmare for a neural-link system like a Neurohelmet. A MechWarrior's brain maps easily to human shapes (Biped) or animal shapes (Quad). Controlling an odd, asymmetrical number of limbs would cause severe mental strain. A Pentapod would almost certainly require a Multi-Pilot Cockpit (similar to Tripods) to divide the piloting and gunnery load.Part 2: Community-Acceptable Alpha Strike RulesTo be accepted by the community, custom rules must not be "overpowered" or "broken." They should adapt existing traits from Quads and Tripods.Here is how you can define them using Alpha Strike's framework:1. Movement & Motive TypeDesignation: Use (H) for Hexapod and (P) for Pentapod.Terrain Costs: They treat terrain exactly like standard Ground/Biped 'Mechs.The "Hull Down" Ability: Like Quads, Hexapods and Pentapods can go "Hull Down" to gain partial cover anywhere, representing their ability to crouch their chassis low to the ground.2. Special Ability: Redundant Actuators (HEX / PENT)In Alpha Strike, critical hits to the "Motive" system or "Legs" slow a 'Mech down. More legs mean more redundancy.PENT (Pentapod): Ignore the first MV (Movement) critical hit or Motive damage result taken during a game.HEX (Hexapod): Ignore the first two MV critical hits or Motive damage results taken during a game.3. They can fire into their rear arc with no secondary target modifier or penalty.4.
