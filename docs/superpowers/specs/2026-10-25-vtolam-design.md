# VTOLAM (Ideas #81)

**Source:** BishopSteiner, "LOCUST VTOLAM concept" (2021), DeviantArt:
https://www.deviantart.com/bishopsteiner/art/LOCUST-VTOLAM-concept-899660337. These are fan rules, "by no means official". The
author tags the design "Experimental Tech (Illegal) 3150".

**Rules level:** Custom Homebrew (6), like the Omni-LAM.

## The author's rules (as built)

- **Form:** a Bimodal LAM whose second mode is a VTOL. It has 'Mech and VTOL modes and no AirMech mode.
- **Conversion equipment:** 20% of tonnage, rounded up like the other LAM conversion weights.
- **Maximum weight:** 30 tons, the VTOL limit. The creator caps the tonnage list and clamps the tonnage on switching, and reports a violation if the tonnage is over.
- **Jump jets:** none required. The LAM minimum of 3 Jump MP does not apply.
- **Engines and gyros:** any fusion engine (standard, compact, light, XL, XXL; Inner Sphere or Clan) and any gyro. The other LAM armor and structure limits stay.
- **Fuel:** none is needed for flight. Fuel points are 0, and fuel tanks and bomb bays are not offered.
- **Landing gear:** retractable skids, one slot in each side torso and none in the center torso. They are named "VTOLAM Landing Skids". The avionics are unchanged.
- **Rotor:** in 'Mech mode the rotor armor is the rear center torso. 2 points of damage there destroy the rotors and end conversion. This is shown in the creator and in play; the record sheet is not marked.
- **VTOL movement:** cruise MP = floor((engine rating + VTOL suspension factor) / tonnage), and flank MP = cruise × 1.5, rounded up. The author's figures check out: 25 tons with a 200 engine gives 13/20.

## Our calls

- **Cost:** the conversion cost uses the Bimodal ×0.65, because the VTOLAM is "a form of Bimodal LAM".
- **Battle Value:** marked provisional. It is figured as a Bimodal LAM, with the 'Mech TMM and no VTOL-mode term.
- **Play mode:** the VTOL mode uses the "vehicle" transformation mode.
- **Locust sample, LCT-XX:**
  - The author gives 25 tons, 8/12 MP, a 200 engine, and the LCT-1V's medium laser, two machine guns and TAG.
  - Our additions are an XL engine (the only way to carry the 1V's 4 tons of armor), 1 ton of MG ammunition, and the slot layout.
  - The era is ilClan (3150).
  - Its TRO text is a paraphrase of the author's background, with credit and the link in its Notes.
- **Bimodal prototype:** the author's "Bimodal ASF" Locust has no stats, so it is not built.
