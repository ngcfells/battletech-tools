# Technical Readout: Micro-Mechs

A Micro-Mech is a fan-made walker of 10 to 19 tons with a single torso. It is Custom Homebrew (rules level 6),
not canon, and everything this app makes for one is marked NOT OFFICIAL.

## Credits

**Micro-Mechs are the work of Solarmech.** He devised them many years ago and posted his own text, "Micro-Mechs
for BattleTech, Part 1", on DeviantArt on 29 April 2019 so that it would be archived.

## What Sets One Apart

- **One torso.** The three torso locations of a BattleMech are one, with 12 critical slots. The head has 2 slots
  and each limb 3.
- **Built in kilograms.** Weights go in quarter tons. Missile launchers are bought by the tube: an LRM 9 is a
  legal launcher.
- **Four weapons at most**, and none may be split between locations.
- **No heat scale.** A Micro-Mech cannot build up heat.
- **The pilot sits in the torso**, behind a cockpit with 3 armor points of its own. A hit to the head does not
  hurt the pilot.
- **Small and agile.** It is one level tall, counts as a vehicle for stacking and line of sight, gets a bonus on
  Piloting Skill Rolls, and cannot be the target of leg or swarm attacks. It kicks for 1 point and cannot punch,
  push, club or make a Death From Above attack.
- **Not allowed:** anti-missile systems, hatchets, swords, MASC and Triple Strength Myomer.

## In This App

The Micro-Mech Creator is offered at the Custom Homebrew rules level. A design you have saved stays reachable
below that level. A design can be:

- built under either version, with its cost and Battle Value worked out step by step;
- printed on its own record sheet, biped or quad;
- added to a Classic BattleTech roster and played there, with damage, critical hits, pilot hits and ammunition;
- printed as an Alpha Strike card and added to an Alpha Strike roster.

### Carried by DropShips

Version 6.1.0.2's AeroTech 2 supplement gives Micro-Mechs a bay of their own. The **Micro-Mech Bay** weighs 75
tons, carries one Micro-Mech of up to 19 tons, holds basic repair facilities and quarters for its pilot and a
technician, and costs 15,000 C-bills. No BattleMech fits one. It is offered in the Small Craft / DropShip and
capital ship creators at the Custom Homebrew level. The text adds an emergency rule: two Micro-Mechs can squeeze
into one BattleMech bay, but they deploy at half speed and cannot make an orbital drop.

### Waiting for Their Own Tools

Version 6.1.0.2 also converts Micro-Mechs for other games. This app plays Classic BattleTech and Alpha Strike, each
in its own section. These appendices wait for a section of their own:

- **BattleForce 2:** class Mi, structure always 1, no overheat, a kick of 1 at Point Blank range, and the ranges of
  the weapons BattleForce 2 does not list.
- **Classic BattleTech RPG:** a Vehicle trait for 10 to 19 tons, Micro-Mech piloting, gunnery and tactics skills, a
  Micro-Mechwarrior Training field and life path, and its training events.
- **AeroTech 2:** the bay above is built for construction; the emergency rule waits for an aerospace play tool.

## Alpha Strike Rules

**NOT OFFICIAL.** No rulebook and no Micro-Mech text has Alpha Strike rules for a Micro-Mech. Version 6.1.0.2
converts to BattleForce 2 only. These rules are this app's own. They follow the Alpha Strike Companion's
conversion (pp. 92-141, with errata v1.6) wherever it can be followed, and the play rules of the Micro-Mech texts.

### The Card

| Stat | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **Type** | MM (Micro-Mech) |
| **Size** | 1 |
| **Move** | 2 inches for each Walking MP. A jumping Micro-Mech adds the j code, or prints both rates when they differ. |
| **Armor** | All armor points, divided by 30 and rounded. The cockpit's 3 points are not counted. |
| **Structure** | Always 1 |
| **Overheat** | None |
| **Damage** | The sum of the weapons' values at each range. Above 0 and under half a point is 0*; anything else is rounded up. |
| **Point Value** | Worked out as for a BattleMech |

### Damage

- A weapon that a rulebook converts uses the rulebook's value.
- A launcher with a tube count no rulebook prints (an LRM 9, an SRM 3) uses its family's value for one missile
  hit, times the Cluster Hits Table at a roll of 7 for that many tubes. With Artemis IV the roll is 9.
- The recoilless rifles, the light and heavy mortar and the grenade launcher are in no rulebook's conversion
  tables. Each does a tenth of its damage at every range bracket its range reaches, halved at Short if it has a
  minimum range.
- A weapon with fewer than 10 shots does three quarters of its value (20 shots for an Ultra autocannon, 60 for a
  Rotary). A weapon with no ammunition does nothing.
- The kick is not part of the damage values. A standard physical attack does damage equal to Size, which is 1.

### The MCR Special Ability

Every Micro-Mech card carries **MCR** (Micro-Mech). It stands for these rules:

- **Low profile:** the unit is 1 inch (1 level) tall, as a vehicle, for line of sight. It never has partial cover.
- **Stacking:** it counts as a vehicle for stacking and displacement.
- **No heat:** it has no Heat Scale and no Overheat value. A Heat (HT) attack against it does its heat points as
  damage.
- **Too small to swarm:** it cannot be the target of an anti-'Mech (AM) infantry attack.
- **Light frame:** it may make a standard physical attack (damage 1) or a charge. It may not make a Death From
  Above or a melee attack.
- **Critical hits:** roll on the 'Mech column. On the first Engine Hit, a unit with ENE can make no more weapon
  attacks, and any other unit treats it as a Weapon Hit. A second Engine Hit destroys the unit.
- **Unsealed:** without the SEAL ability it follows the IndustrialMech limits for deep water and vacuum.

### Codes on a Micro-Mech Card

MM and MCR are this app's own. The others are standard Alpha Strike special abilities and mean what the Alpha
Strike rulebook says they mean.

| Code | Meaning | Given by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **MM** | Unit type: Micro-Mech | Every Micro-Mech |
| **MCR** | Micro-Mech rules (above) | Every Micro-Mech |
| **ENE** | Energy: carries nothing that can explode | No ammunition and no one-shot launcher |
| **CASE, CASEII** | Protected ammunition | CASE in the torso, or in every location that holds ammunition |
| **SEAL** | Environmental sealing | A sealed design |
| **IF#** | Indirect fire | LRMs and other indirect-fire launchers that reach Long range |
| **LRM, SRM, AC, FLK** | Damage of that weapon class | At least 1 point of it at Medium range (any flak) |
| **HT** | Heat | Flamers with 5 or more heat points between them |
| **ECM, AECM** | Electronic countermeasures | Guardian or Angel ECM |
| **PRB, BH, RCN** | Probe, Bloodhound, recon | An active probe (RCN comes with either) |
| **TAG** | Target acquisition gear | TAG |
| **C3M, C3S, C3I** | C3 network | A C3 master, slave or C3i computer |
| **SNARC, INARC** | Narc beacons | A Narc or iNarc launcher |
| **JMPW#, JMPS#** | Jumps worse or better than it walks | A jump rate in a different movement bracket |
